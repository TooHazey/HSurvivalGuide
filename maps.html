Interactive Maps

OpenStreetMap-based interactive map for planning routes, identifying terrain, water sources, and landmarks. Click and drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Click markers for details.

Requires internet for tiles. For offline use, download maps via OsmAnd, Organic Maps, or StreetZim (Kiwix) and use the Navigation page principles with a paper map + compass.

How to Use This Map

  • Pan and zoom to your area of interest (default is near Michigan / Great Lakes region).
  • Look for water bodies, roads, elevation changes (contours not shown here; use topographic layers if available), and potential shelter sites.
  • Add your own mental notes or print screenshots for offline reference.
  • For military-style gridded maps, combine with FM 3-25.26 principles from the Navigation page.

Offline Map Options

  • OsmAnd or Organic Maps — Download offline vector maps for your region.
  • Kiwix StreetZim — Offline street maps in ZIM format.
  • USGS Topographic Maps — Free downloads from USGS National Map.
  • Paper maps + compass remain the most reliable backup.
