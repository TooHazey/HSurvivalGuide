Food

Caloric intake becomes critical after the first few days. Focus on reliable, high-calorie sources and basic food production.

Immediate Food Sources

  • Insects — High protein; avoid brightly colored or foul-smelling ones. Roast or boil.
  • Fish and small game — Snares, traps, and improvised fishing gear (see Army survival manuals for diagrams).
  • Edible plants — Only eat what you can positively identify. Universal edibility test exists but is slow and risky.
  • Stored food — Prioritize calorie-dense items (rice, beans, oats, oils, nuts).

Basic Farming Priorities

  1. Calorie crops: potatoes, sweet potatoes, corn, beans, squash
  2. Fast-growing greens: lettuce, spinach, radishes, herbs
  3. Perennials once established: fruit trees, berries, asparagus
  4. Seed saving: learn to save seeds from open-pollinated varieties

Soil, water, and sun are the three non-negotiables. Start small and expand.

Minimum Daily Calories

  • Sedentary: ~1,800–2,200 kcal
  • Active / manual labor: 2,500–3,500+ kcal
  • Prioritize fats and carbohydrates for energy density

Primary sources: ATP 3-50.21 and USDA gardening publications.
