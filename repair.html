Repair & Tools

Fixing what you already have is often more valuable than new gear. Focus on multi-use tools and field-expedient repairs drawn from military and practical fieldcraft.

Essential Tool Kit

  • Cutting — Fixed-blade knife (full tang preferred), folding saw, axe/hatchet
  • Fastening — Paracord or bank line (550 cord), duct tape, wire, nails, screws, zip ties
  • Prying / Digging — Pry bar or sturdy stick, shovel or digging tool
  • Striking — Hammer or mallet (or improvised with rock + wood)
  • Sharpening — Whetstone, diamond stone, or ceramic rod
  • Measuring — Tape measure or marked stick; knowledge of body measurements (hand span, pace)

Common Field Repairs

  • Clothing & packs — Sewing kit (needles, heavy thread, patches). Learn running stitch, whip stitch, and how to replace a zipper slider. Reinforce high-wear areas.
  • Containers & canteens — Patch plastic with duct tape or hot glue. Metal containers can be sealed with epoxy, solder, or temporary tape. Test for leaks.
  • Tool handles — Replace broken handles with hardwood, shape with knife, secure with wedges and epoxy or hide glue. Drill or burn a hole for hanging.
  • Shelter frames — Lash poles with cordage (square lashing, diagonal lashing). Notching and wedges for stronger joints when time allows.
  • Footwear — Repair soles with epoxy or tire patches. Replace laces with paracord. Dry thoroughly to prevent rot.

Improvised Techniques

  • Unravel paracord for sewing thread or fishing line
  • Use inner bark, vines, or grass for temporary natural cordage
  • Duct tape for sealing, binding, and temporary splints
  • Wedge and lash for most structural repairs
  • Keep a small hardware kit: assorted screws, washers, wire, safety pins

Maintenance Habits

  • Sharpen tools after every use
  • Oil metal surfaces to prevent rust
  • Inspect gear regularly and repair small issues before they become big ones
  • Practice repairs at home so muscle memory is there when needed

Primary sources: U.S. Army field manuals and general fieldcraft principles.
