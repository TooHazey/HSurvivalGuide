Repair & Tools

Being able to fix what you have is often more valuable than having new gear. Focus on multi-use tools and basic repair skills.

Essential Tool Categories

  • Cutting — Fixed-blade knife, folding saw, axe or hatchet
  • Fastening — Cordage (paracord, bank line), duct tape, wire, nails/screws
  • Prying & Digging — Pry bar, shovel, or sturdy stick
  • Striking — Hammer or mallet
  • Measuring — Tape measure or marked stick

Common Repairs

  • Clothing & gear — Sewing kit (needles, strong thread, patches). Learn basic stitches and how to repair tears and zippers.
  • Containers — Patch plastic with duct tape or plastic welding. Metal can be sealed with epoxy or solder if available.
  • Tools — Sharpen knives and axes regularly. Replace handles on broken tools using wood and wedges.
  • Structures — Lash poles with cordage for temporary frames. Use wedges and notching for more permanent joints.

Improvised Solutions

  • Paracord can replace many fasteners and be unraveled for thread
  • Duct tape is temporary but highly useful for sealing and binding
  • Natural cordage (inner bark, vines, grass) for non-critical uses
  • Keep a small assortment of hardware (screws, washers, wire)

Primary sources: U.S. Army field manuals and general fieldcraft knowledge.
