Water

Safe water is your highest daily priority after shelter. The average person needs about 3–4 liters per day in moderate conditions (more in heat or during hard work).

Finding Water

  • Streams, rivers, and lakes (always purify)
  • Morning dew on vegetation (cloth or plastic sheet)
  • Rain collection (clean containers or tarps)
  • Plant sources (certain vines, banana trees — identify carefully)
  • Digging in dry stream beds or at the base of hills

Never drink untreated surface water if you can avoid it. Pathogens and chemical contamination are common risks.

Purification Methods (in order of preference)

  1. Boiling — Rolling boil for 1 minute (3 minutes above 6,500 ft). Most reliable method.
  2. Chemical treatment — Unscented household bleach (2 drops per liter of clear water, 4 for cloudy; wait 30 minutes) or iodine tablets.
  3. Filtration — Commercial filters or improvised (cloth + sand/charcoal layers). Filtration alone does not kill viruses.
  4. Solar disinfection (SODIS) — Clear PET bottles in direct sun for 6+ hours (longer if cloudy).

Storage

  • Use food-grade containers only
  • Store in a cool, dark place
  • Rotate stored water every 6–12 months
  • Label containers with date filled

Primary source: U.S. Army ATP 3-50.21 (Survival) and related field guidance.
