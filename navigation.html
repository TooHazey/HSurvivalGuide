Navigation

Knowing where you are and how to move deliberately is essential. Map and compass skills remain valuable even with GPS.

Map Basics

  • Contour lines show elevation and terrain shape
  • Scale tells you distance
  • Grid systems (MGRS or UTM) allow precise location
  • Legend explains symbols

Compass Use

  1. Orient the map to the ground
  2. Take a bearing to a landmark
  3. Follow the bearing, accounting for declination
  4. Pace count or time/distance for dead reckoning

Without Tools

  • Sun rises in the east, sets in the west
  • Shadow stick method for direction
  • North Star (Polaris) in the Northern Hemisphere
  • Terrain association: ridges, valleys, waterways

Primary source: FM 3-25.26 Map Reading and Land Navigation (U.S. Army).
