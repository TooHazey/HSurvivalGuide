Navigation

Knowing where you are and how to move deliberately is essential. Map and compass skills remain valuable even with GPS. Use the Interactive Maps page for online planning, then apply these principles offline.

Map Basics

  • Contour lines show elevation and terrain shape
  • Scale tells you distance
  • Grid systems (MGRS or UTM) allow precise location
  • Legend explains symbols
  • Orient the map to the ground using a compass or known landmarks

Compass Use

  1. Orient the map to the ground
  2. Take a bearing to a landmark
  3. Follow the bearing, accounting for declination (magnetic variation)
  4. Pace count or time/distance for dead reckoning

Without Tools

  • Sun rises in the east, sets in the west
  • Shadow stick method for direction
  • North Star (Polaris) in the Northern Hemisphere
  • Terrain association: ridges, valleys, waterways, and distinctive features
