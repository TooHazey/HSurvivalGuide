Shelter & Fire

Exposure is a faster killer than hunger. Shelter and fire are your primary defenses against cold, wind, and rain.

Shelter Priorities

  • Get out of the wind and off the ground
  • Insulate from the ground (debris, sleeping pad, or raised platform)
  • Waterproof roof that sheds water
  • Ventilation to reduce condensation

Simple options: debris hut, lean-to, tarp shelter, snow cave (in winter), or using existing structures.

Fire

Fire provides heat, light, cooking, water purification, and signaling.

Fire Triangle

  • Heat (spark or flame)
  • Fuel (tinder → kindling → fuel wood)
  • Oxygen

Basic Fire Lay

  1. Prepare tinder (dry grass, birch bark, cotton, etc.)
  2. Add small dry kindling
  3. Gradually add larger fuel
  4. Protect from wind and rain

Key Skills

  • Practice fire starting before you need it
  • Carry multiple ignition methods
  • Know how to collect dry tinder in wet conditions
